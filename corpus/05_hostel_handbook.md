# Nexus University Hostel Handbook
## Academic Year 2026-2027

This Hostel Handbook governs residence in University hostels on the main campus. Occupation of a room is a licence, not a tenancy. Residents remain subject to the Student Discipline Code and to campus safety directions issued by the Chief Warden.

## Section 12.1 Weekday Curfew and Entry

Hostel gates close according to the published in-time. On weekdays, the hostel curfew is 10:00 PM. Residents must be inside their allotted hostel by 10:00 PM Sunday through Thursday. After curfew, entry is recorded as late entry. Repeated late entry without prior permission from the warden may lead to a written warning, informing of parents or guardians, and, after three recorded late entries in a semester, referral to the Hostel Disciplinary Committee.

Night roll is taken after curfew. A resident who is absent without leave is treated as missing for safety purposes and the warden may notify campus security. The weekday curfew of 10:00 PM applies during teaching weeks, mid-semester tests, and end-semester examinations unless the Chief Warden posts a special circular for a named event.

## Section 12.2 Weekend and Holiday Hours

On Fridays and Saturdays, and on nights before a declared University holiday, in-time is 11:00 PM unless a more restrictive circular is issued during a security alert. Weekend hours do not authorise overnight absence from the city. Residents who wish to leave campus overnight must file a leave application.

## Section 12.3 Leave from Hostel

Leave of one night or more requires an online application approved by the warden. For residents below eighteen years of age, parent confirmation is required. Leave to stay with local relatives is not automatic; an address and contact number are mandatory. Guest rooms, if any, are booked through the Hostel Office for limited visiting hours only.

## Section 12.4 Rooms, Inventory, and Energy

Rooms are allotted by the Hostel Office. Unauthorised room changes, extra furniture, cooking coils, and high-load appliances are prohibited. Residents are jointly responsible for common-area damage on their floor if individual responsibility cannot be established. Lights and fans shall be switched off when leaving. Quiet hours begin at 11:00 PM in corridors even when weekend in-time is later.

## Section 12.5 Visitors

Day visitors may be received in the designated lobby between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM after entry in the visitor register. Visitors are not permitted in residential corridors of another gender's hostel. Delivery personnel shall leave parcels at the gate office.

## Section 12.6 Mess and Hygiene

Mess membership follows the hostel allotment. Temporary mess-off requires application three working days in advance and is limited to the days published by the mess committee. Wasting food, entering the kitchen without authorisation, and removing utensils is prohibited. Ill residents should inform the warden so that a sick-diet arrangement can be considered; this is not a medical exemption from academic rules.

## Section 12.7 Safety, Substances, and Conduct

Possession or use of tobacco, alcohol, or illegal substances in hostels is a serious offence. Ragging in any form is prohibited and is reported under national anti-ragging regulations as well as University discipline. Fire equipment shall not be tampered with. Parking of two-wheelers is allowed only in marked bays. Security staff directions at the gate are binding.

## Section 12.8 Vacating and Summer Stay

Residents vacate within twenty-four hours after the last examination of the even semester unless summer stay is sanctioned for internship or supplementary examinations. Personal property left behind may be stored for thirty days and then disposed of. Room inspection deductions are taken from the hostel deposit.

## Section 12.9 Complaints

Maintenance complaints are lodged on the hostel portal. Harassment complaints may be made to the warden, the Chief Warden, or the Internal Complaints Committee as applicable. Retaliation against a complainant is an independent offence.

## Section 12.10 Relationship to Campus Discipline

Hostel in-time is a residence rule. Campus-wide conduct rules appear in the Student Discipline policy. Residents must follow both. Where a safety emergency exists, the Chief Warden may lock gates earlier than the ordinary curfew and shall record the reason.
