# Nexus University Scholarship Policy
## Academic Year 2026-2027

This Scholarship Policy describes merit, need-linked, and special awards administered by Nexus University for Academic Year 2026-2027. External government scholarships follow the rules of the awarding body; the University only facilitates verification.

## Section 18.1 Categories of Award

The University administers (a) Merit Scholarships based on prior academic performance, (b) Need-Linked Tuition Support based on documented family income, (c) Sports and Cultural Awards for University representation at specified levels, and (d) Donor Awards bound by gift agreements. A student may hold more than one award only if the combined tuition waiver does not exceed one hundred percent of billed tuition and the donor agreement allows stacking.

## Section 18.2 Merit Scholarship Criteria

Continuing undergraduate Merit Scholarships require a cumulative grade point average of at least 8.5 and no F grade in the review period, together with good disciplinary standing. First-year entry merit awards follow the admission rank lists published by the Admissions Office. Merit awards are reviewed every even semester. A fall in cumulative grade point average below 8.0 leads to discontinuation of the merit award from the following semester.

## Section 18.3 Need-Linked Support

Need-linked support requires income documents specified in the annual circular, typically including a recent income certificate and bank statements. False disclosure is disciplinary misconduct and leads to recovery of funds. Need-linked support does not waive hostel mess charges unless the award letter says so. Deadlines for need-linked applications are announced on the portal; late applications are not considered except where the circular allows a documented portal outage.

## Section 18.4 Sports and Cultural Awards

Awards require a recommendation from the Director of Physical Education or the Cultural Coordinator and evidence of representation. Doping violations or match-fixing allegations under inquiry suspend the award. These awards do not create attendance waivers beyond duty leave granted under the Attendance Policy.

## Section 18.5 Donor Awards

Donor awards may include additional essay, interview, or service conditions. The University will not alter academic grading to satisfy a donor. If a donor condition conflicts with University non-discrimination policy, the University condition prevails and the award may be declined.

## Section 18.6 Continuation, Withdrawal, and Bond

Awards may be withdrawn for academic misconduct, ragging findings, or fee default that is not cured. Some professional programme awards include a service bond as stated in the award letter. This policy does not authorise conversion of a scholarship into cash for personal travel, nor payment of the award to a third party.

## Section 18.7 Application and Appeals

Students apply on the scholarship portal. Decisions are communicated by email. Appeals on procedure, not on academic judgement of merit rank, may be filed within ten days to the Dean of Academic Affairs. There is no walk-in allotment of donor seats in departmental offices.

## Section 18.8 Interaction with Fees

Scholarship disbursement is ordinarily an internal credit against tuition. Until the credit appears, the student should follow Fee Policy deadlines or request a documented bridging note from the Scholarship Cell. A bridging note is not available merely because an application is pending.

## Section 18.9 Records

The Scholarship Cell retains applications for the period required by audit rules. Students should keep copies. This policy does not create a scholarship for every student with a high grade; awards are limited by budget.
