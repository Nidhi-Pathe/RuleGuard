# Nexus University Fee Policy
## Academic Year 2026-2027

This Fee Policy states tuition, examination, hostel, and related charges, and the University's rules on payment, late fees, and refunds. Amounts may be revised by the Finance Committee; the figures below are those notified for Academic Year 2026-2027 unless a later official circular supersedes them. Payments are accepted by the methods listed in Section 9.4.

## Section 9.1 Tuition and Programme Fees

Undergraduate tuition for regular engineering and science programmes is payable each semester as billed on the student portal. Postgraduate tuition follows the offer letter. Scholarships, if awarded, are adjustments to the billed amount and do not themselves create a right to delay payment of the residual dues. A student who has not paid tuition is not in good financial standing and may be denied course registration, examination hall tickets, and hostel occupancy.

The University does not reduce tuition because a student attends fewer electives than the maximum load. Tuition is a programme semester charge, not a per-credit invoice, except for approved part-time postgraduate schemes that are billed per credit in the offer letter.

## Section 9.2 Other Mandatory Charges

In addition to tuition, students pay an examination fee per registered end-semester session, a library and information resources fee, a student activity fee, and, where applicable, laboratory consumable charges notified by the department. Identity-card replacement, condonation, re-totaling, and duplicate transcript fees are published in the portal fee catalogue. Unofficial collections by student clubs cannot be billed as University fees.

## Section 9.3 Payment Deadlines

For Academic Year 2026-2027, undergraduate tuition for the Odd Semester is due on 15 August 2026. This deadline applies to all continuing undergraduate students in regular programmes. New first-year students follow the date printed on the admission offer, which shall not be later than the date of commencement of instruction. Even Semester undergraduate tuition is due on 10 January 2027. Examination fees for each session are due not later than seven days before the first paper of that session.

Hostel rent, where the student is an alloted resident, is due on the same date as tuition for that semester unless the Hostel Office issues a separate instalment plan in writing. Late payment attracts the late fee in Section 9.5. Students waiting for a sanctioned education loan must upload the loan-sanction letter before the due date to request a short administrative extension; an extension is not automatic.

## Section 9.4 Permitted Payment Methods

Fees shall be paid through the University payment gateway using net banking, debit card, credit card, or Unified Payments Interface, or by demand draft drawn in favour of Nexus University as specified on the portal. Cash is not accepted at departmental offices. A payment is complete only when the portal shows a success receipt with a University transaction identifier.

## Section 9.5 Late Fees and Debarment

A late fee of two percent of the overdue tuition component accrues for the first week after the deadline, and an additional one percent may accrue for each subsequent week up to a published cap. After four weeks of default without an approved extension, the University may debar the student from classes and examinations until dues are cleared. Partial payments are credited first to the oldest tuition component.

## Section 9.6 Refunds and Withdrawal

A student who withdraws before the commencement of instruction may receive a refund of tuition after deduction of the processing amount stated in the admission brochure. After instruction begins, refunds follow a sliding scale published by the Finance Office and do not include non-refundable registration charges. Hostel refunds depend on occupancy and notice. Scholarship amounts already credited may be recovered if the student becomes ineligible.

## Section 9.7 Fee Concessions

Fee concession is distinct from scholarship. Concession on humanitarian grounds, if any, is decided by a committee chaired by the Registrar and is documented. Staff-ward concessions follow a separate ordinance. No teacher or warden may privately waive a fee.

## Section 9.8 Records and Receipts

Students shall retain electronic receipts. Disputes about payment must be raised within thirty days with bank evidence. The University is not responsible for payments made to unofficial accounts advertised on messaging groups.

## Section 9.9 Relationship to Other Policies

Financial standing does not replace academic eligibility. A student who has paid fees but has an attendance shortage remains ineligible under the Attendance Policy. A student who has academic eligibility but unpaid examination fees remains ineligible under the Examination Rules. The fee deadline in Section 9.3 is the rule applied by the Finance Office for Odd Semester undergraduate tuition unless a later Finance Committee circular expressly replaces that date.
