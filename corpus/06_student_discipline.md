# Nexus University Student Discipline Code
## Academic Year 2026-2027

This Student Discipline Code states expected conduct on campus, in hostels, online University forums, and University-sponsored off-campus events. It also describes inquiries and penalties. Academic evaluation remains governed by the Academic Regulations and Examination Rules; this Code addresses behaviour.

## Section 15.1 General Conduct

Students shall treat staff, visitors, and fellow students with respect. Intimidation, hate speech, harassment, and discrimination on prohibited grounds are offences. Students shall comply with lawful directions of University officials during emergencies. Posting confidential examination material or another student's private data on public platforms is misconduct.

## Section 15.2 Identity, Access, and Property

Students shall not lend identity cards, tailgate through access gates, or enter restricted laboratories without authorisation. Theft, vandalism, and unauthorised use of University computing resources are offences. Lost property should be deposited with campus security.

## Section 15.3 Academic Integrity Outside the Hall

Plagiarism, collusion, fabrication of data, and unauthorized contract cheating in assignments are academic misconduct even when they occur outside an examination hall. Instructors shall report suspected cases to the Head of Department. Penalties may include zero for the assessment, failure in the course, and a disciplinary annotation. Repeated offences may lead to suspension.

## Section 15.4 Ragging, Harassment, and Safety

Ragging is prohibited. Sexual harassment complaints follow the University policy implementing applicable law. Students must not possess weapons. Drone flying over hostels or examination buildings without authorisation is prohibited.

## Section 15.5 Alcohol, Tobacco, and Controlled Substances

The campus is tobacco-free in academic buildings. Alcohol is not permitted in hostels or undergraduate events. Controlled substances are prohibited. Being intoxicated on campus and unable to take safety directions is an offence even if consumption occurred off campus.

## Section 15.6 Campus Return Hours for Resident Students

Resident students are required to return to their hostels by 11:00 PM on weekdays. This campus conduct rule is intended to reduce late-night risk on internal roads and to support a consistent security deployment at hostel gates. Security officers may stop a resident student on campus after 11:00 PM on a weekday and record the student as being outside hostel in-time. The 11:00 PM weekday return obligation applies during teaching weeks. Special late permissions, if granted for a University event, must be carried in writing.

Resident students found repeatedly on campus after 11:00 PM on weekdays without permission may be fined and referred to the Student Disciplinary Committee. This section is a binding conduct requirement for hostel residents and is used by campus security in incident reports.

## Section 15.7 Demonstrations and Public Expression

Peaceful assembly in designated areas is allowed with prior intimation to the Dean of Student Affairs when more than fifty participants are expected. Blocking gates, occupying offices, and using amplification after 9:00 PM near hostels is not permitted. Political campaigning by external parties on campus requires Registrar approval.

## Section 15.8 Digital Conduct

University email and learning platforms are official channels. Impersonating staff online, sharing login credentials, and conducting cyberbullying against members of the University community are offences. Students should not assume that deleted messages are untraceable on University systems.

## Section 15.9 Inquiries and Penalties

A show-cause notice shall state the alleged facts. The student may submit a written defence and may be heard in person. Penalties include warning, fine, community restitution, withdrawal of facilities, suspension, and rustication. Interim suspension may be used where safety is at risk. Appeals lie to the Vice-Chancellor's nominee within fifteen days.

## Section 15.10 Committees

The Student Disciplinary Committee includes faculty members and, except in cases of sexual harassment or ragging where separate statutory committees apply, may include a student representative who is not connected to the incident. The committee shall record reasons. This Code does not authorise student committees to impose unofficial punishments.

## Section 15.11 Relationship to Hostel Rules

Hostel residents must also follow the Hostel Handbook. Security incident reports may cite this Code, the handbook, or both. Students seeking clarification of night hours should read Section 15.6 together with hostel entry rules and, if the texts appear difficult to reconcile, request written guidance from the Dean of Student Affairs.
