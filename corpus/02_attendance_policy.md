# Nexus University Attendance Policy
## Academic Year 2026-2027

This Attendance Policy explains how class presence is recorded, how shortages are notified, and how attendance relates to examination eligibility. It applies to lectures, tutorials, laboratories, studios, and mandatory workshops that appear on the official timetable. The policy should be read with the Examination Rules and the Medical Exemption Rules.

## Section 4.1 Recording of Attendance

Attendance is marked in the student information system at the start of each scheduled session. For a lecture of fifty minutes, a student who arrives more than ten minutes late may be marked absent at the instructor's discretion, which shall be applied consistently to the whole class. Laboratory attendance requires presence for the full session unless the instructor records an authorised early departure. Proxy attendance, signing for another student, or sharing a login session to falsify presence is academic misconduct.

Biometric or smart-card readers, where installed, are the primary record. If a reader fails, the instructor's signed paper roll, uploaded the same day, is accepted. Students who believe a session was marked incorrectly must raise a correction request within three working days. Requests raised after that period are not entertained except where the system was demonstrably unavailable.

## Section 4.2 Attendance Requirements

Students require at least 75% attendance in each registered course to appear for the semester examination of that course. Attendance is calculated as the number of sessions attended divided by the number of sessions held, multiplied by one hundred, and rounded to two decimal places. The 75% threshold applies to lectures and to laboratories separately where a course has both components with independent session counts. Students below the threshold are notified as having an attendance shortage and are not issued an examination hall ticket for the affected course.

Departments shall compute attendance one week before the start of end-semester examinations and freeze the figure after a two-day correction window. No instructor may privately lower the 75% rule for an individual student. Condonation, if any, follows only the procedures in Section 4.5 of this policy and does not replace the ordinary 75% rule for students without an approved pathway.

## Section 4.3 Shortage Notices and Monitoring

When a student's attendance in a course falls below eighty percent at mid-semester, the Department Office shall send a caution notice to the student portal and to the registered email address. A second notice is sent if attendance falls below seventy-five percent before the freeze date. Failure to read a notice is not an excuse. Mentors shall discuss attendance with students on warning or probation. Parents or guardians listed in the enrolment record may be informed when attendance in two or more courses is below seventy percent.

## Section 4.4 University Duty and Representational Absence

Students deputed by the University to represent Nexus University in official sports, cultural, or academic events may receive duty leave. Duty leave sessions are excluded from the denominator of the attendance calculation for the dates named in the deputation letter. Informal club travel without a deputation letter does not qualify. The maximum duty-leave exclusion in a semester is ten teaching days unless the Dean of Student Affairs authorises a larger exclusion for national-level representation.

## Section 4.5 Condonation of Shortage

A student whose attendance is below 75% but not below 70%, and who has no disciplinary probation, may apply for condonation on payment of the condonation fee published for the semester. Condonation is not a right. The Head of Department may refuse condonation where the student has a pattern of unexplained absence. Condonation cannot be combined with an unapproved late medical claim. Students below 70% without an approved medical exemption remain ineligible for the end-semester examination in that course.

## Section 4.6 Laboratories, Studios, and Clinical Sessions

Practical courses require presence because skills cannot be demonstrated by reading alone. Missing more than the permitted fraction of laboratory sessions may make the student ineligible for the practical examination even if theory attendance is adequate. Make-up laboratory sessions, if offered, are scheduled at the department's convenience and are not guaranteed. Safety briefing attendance is compulsory; a student who misses the briefing may be barred from the laboratory until the briefing is completed.

## Section 4.7 Online and Hybrid Sessions

If a course is approved for hybrid delivery, online presence counts only when the student joins with the registered account, keeps the camera policy of the course, and remains for the required duration. Connection failure is not automatically excused. Students must notify the instructor during the session or immediately after with evidence of an outage. Recorded lectures, where provided as a supplement, do not themselves create attendance credit unless the course plan states a verified viewing requirement.

## Section 4.8 Special Categories

Students with documented disabilities may receive reasonable adjustments to the method of recording attendance, such as a designated seat or assistance with card tap, but the 75% quantitative requirement in Section 4.2 remains the default eligibility rule unless a specific adjustment is written into an approved accessibility plan. Pregnant students and student-parents may request timetable consideration; such requests do not by themselves alter the percentage threshold.

## Section 4.9 Responsibilities of Instructors

Instructors shall close attendance within twenty-four hours of a session. They shall not mark a student present as a favour. They shall publish a running attendance view on the portal or an equivalent channel at least twice in the semester before the freeze. Failure of an instructor to mark a session is corrected by the Head of Department using the timetable as evidence that the session was held.

## Section 4.10 Interpretation of Attendance Disputes

Disputes about arithmetic shall be resolved from system logs. Disputes about whether a session was held shall be resolved from the official timetable and the instructor's session plan.
