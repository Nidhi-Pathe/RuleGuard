# Nexus University Examination Rules
## Academic Year 2026-2027

These Examination Rules govern eligibility, conduct, evaluation, and results for end-semester and supplementary examinations conducted by Nexus University. The Controller of Examinations is responsible for operational delivery. Academic judgements on content remain with examiners appointed by Boards of Studies.

## Section 5.1 Examination Eligibility

A student may be issued a hall ticket only if the student is registered for the course, has no unpaid examination fee for that session, and meets the attendance requirement applicable to the course under the Attendance Policy. Students barred for attendance shortage, pending disciplinary suspension, or missing identity verification cannot enter the examination hall. Eligibility lists are published at least four working days before the first examination of the session.

Possession of a hall ticket does not override a later discovery of ineligibility caused by fee default or a proven attendance falsification. In such cases the script, if written, may be cancelled.

## Section 5.2 Hall Tickets and Identity

Hall tickets are available on the student portal. Students must carry the hall ticket and the University identity card to every session. A temporary identity slip issued on the same day by Student Services may be accepted once in a semester if the identity card is lost and a complaint number is produced. Photograph mismatch or impersonation is referred immediately to the flying squad and to the disciplinary authority.

## Section 5.3 Examination Conduct

Candidates shall occupy the allotted seat fifteen minutes before the start time. Bags, mobile phones, smart watches, programmable calculators (unless permitted for that paper), notes, and Bluetooth devices are prohibited inside the hall. The invigilator's instruction is binding during the session. Communication between candidates, glancing at another script, or writing after time is called constitutes misconduct. Rough work must be done in the answer book.

A candidate who arrives more than thirty minutes late is not admitted. No candidate may leave the hall during the first thirty minutes or the last fifteen minutes of a written paper. Biological breaks are escorted where the duration of the paper exceeds two hours.

If a candidate becomes ill during a paper, the invigilator shall call medical assistance. The candidate may be allowed to continue or to withdraw. Withdrawal does not by itself create a right to a special examination on another day except where the Medical Exemption Rules and a later decision of the Controller of Examinations jointly authorise a make-up sitting. Question papers are not emailed to candidates.

## Section 5.4 Academic Misconduct in Examinations

Copying, possessing unauthorised material, impersonation, leaking a question paper, or using an electronic device to obtain answers is examination malpractice. The invigilator shall seize material, mark the answer book, and submit a report. The Malpractice Committee may impose penalties ranging from cancellation of the paper to cancellation of the semester or rustication, depending on severity and prior record. A student under inquiry may be permitted to write remaining papers provisionally; results remain withheld until the inquiry ends.

## Section 5.5 Medical Situations During an Examination

Illness arising in the hall is managed as an invigilation incident. Make-up sittings exist only when separately authorised in writing after a medical review. This section does not list social or travel reasons for missing a paper.

## Section 5.6 Evaluation, Re-totaling, and Revaluation

End-semester scripts are evaluated by appointed examiners. Results are published on the portal. A student may apply for re-totaling within ten days of result publication on payment of the prescribed fee. Revaluation of theory scripts, where offered for that programme, follows the same window. Practical and viva marks are not revalued except for documented clerical error. The higher mark after revaluation, if the scheme so provides, replaces the original theory mark.

## Section 5.7 Supplementary and Improvement Examinations

Supplementary examinations are conducted for courses in which the student earned F or was absent with approved medical exemption. Improvement examinations, if announced, are limited to courses with pass grades and do not extend programme duration automatically. A student cannot use supplementary sittings to bypass an attendance bar from the original semester; the student must re-register and meet eligibility in a later offering unless a specific make-up eligibility has been granted in writing.

## Section 5.8 Internal Assessment and Examination Relationship

Internal assessment marks are submitted by departments before the start of end-semester examinations. The Controller of Examinations shall not accept revised internals after the deadline except to correct a proven portal error. Students must obtain the minimum end-semester mark stated in the Academic Regulations even if internals are high. Conversely, strong examination performance does not replace a missing laboratory internal where the laboratory is a distinct component.

## Section 5.9 Question Papers and Language

Question papers follow the pattern in the approved course file. Unless the programme is taught in another language, papers are in English. Students may request a large-print paper or additional time only under an approved accessibility plan filed with the Controller of Examinations at least three weeks before the session.

## Section 5.10 Degree Certificates and Transcripts

Official transcripts are issued by the Registrar. Degree certificates are conferred at Convocation or collected afterwards according to the Registrar's collection notice. Replacement of a damaged certificate follows the process published by the Registrar's office.

## Section 5.11 Flying Squad and Surveillance

The University may deploy a flying squad and closed-circuit cameras in examination buildings. Recordings are used only for integrity inquiries and are retained for the period stated in the data-protection notice. Tampering with cameras or obstructing a squad member is a separate offence.

## Section 5.12 Interpretation

The Controller of Examinations interprets operational examination questions. Academic content disputes go to the Board of Studies. These rules do not create examination centres outside the campus except for approved distance-proctored courses that the Academic Council has specifically listed.
